I am an Associate Professor at the College of Software, Nankai University. Previously, I was a Shuimu Scholar postdoctoral researcher at the Pervasive HCI Lab of Tsinghua University, working with A/Prof Chun Yu. I am also a Visiting Fellow at the University of New South Wales (UNSW), Australia.

I am interested in ubiquitous computing, mobile sensing, and human-computer interaction (HCI). In particular, my research uses unobtrusive sensing data to infer human behaviours and mental states (e.g., personality, emotion, learning engagement), to help improve self-awareness and promote human well-being.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at RMIT University in 2022, under the supervision of Prof Flora Salim, Dr Wei Shao and Dr Mohammad Saiedur Rahaman. I obtained my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees from the Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT) in 2016 and 2018. I have published my research in top venues (e.g., PACM IMWUT/UbiComp, Scientific Data), released the public datasets In-Gauge and En-Gage, and won the IMWUT Distinguished Paper Award at UbiComp 2021.

南开大学软件学院副教授。曾任清华大学计算机系 Pervasive HCI 实验室“水木学者”博士后，合作导师为喻纯副教授；同时为澳大利亚新南威尔士大学（UNSW）访问学者。

研究兴趣包括普适计算、移动感知与人机交互（HCI），重点研究如何利用无感（非侵入式）感知数据推断人的行为与心理状态（如人格、情绪、学习投入），帮助人们提升自我觉察、促进身心福祉。

2022 年于澳大利亚皇家墨尔本理工大学（RMIT）获计算机科学博士学位，导师为 Flora Salim 教授、Wei Shao 博士和 Mohammad Saiedur Rahaman 博士；2016 年、2018 年分别于北京理工大学获学士、硕士学位。研究成果发表于 PACM IMWUT/UbiComp、Scientific Data 等顶级期刊与会议，公开发布了 In-Gauge 与 En-Gage 数据集，并获 UbiComp 2021 IMWUT 杰出论文奖。

Research Overview研究方向

My research sits at the intersection of ubiquitous computing and HCI and spans three connected strands: sensing and modelling human behaviour and mental states, understanding people’s needs to inform design, and building interactive systems that support well-being.研究位于普适计算与人机交互的交叉领域，包含三条相互衔接的主线：感知与建模人的行为与心理状态，理解人的需求以指导设计，以及构建促进身心福祉的交互系统。

  • Sensing and Modelling感知与建模

    Modelling behaviour, emotion and mental states from smartphone, wearable and environmental sensing data collected in the wild.利用真实场景中的手机、可穿戴设备与环境传感数据，对人的行为、情绪与心理状态进行建模。

  • Understanding and Design理解与设计

    Empirical studies of how people learn, live and interact at home and in class, informing the design of supportive technologies.通过实证研究理解人在家庭与课堂中的学习、生活与互动，为支持性技术的设计提供依据。

  • Interactive Systems and Intervention交互系统与干预

    Building and evaluating interactive systems, including LLM-based agents, that support mental well-being and everyday life.构建并评估交互系统（包括基于大语言模型的智能体），为心理健康与日常生活提供支持。
